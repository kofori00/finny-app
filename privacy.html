Privacy policy

Finny · last updated 23 October 2026 (Ask Finny added)

Finny is a research app that shows historical statistics about US stocks (odds, strategy fit, news effects). This page explains what personal data the app uses.

What we collect

When you create an account we store your e-mail address, the sign-in method you used (for example Google or e-mail) and the date you signed up. If you sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub we receive only your name and e-mail address from that service; we never see your password. We also store whether your account has been approved.

What we do not collect

We do not collect payment details, contacts, location, or the content of your accounts at other services. The tickers you look up are processed to show results but are not stored with your name.

How we use it

Only to let you sign in, to decide on access, and to contact you about your account if needed. We do not sell or share your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Ask Finny (AI answers)

When you use Ask Finny, your question and the numbers shown for the stock on screen are sent to Google's Gemini service to write the answer, together with passages from Finny's own research. Do not type personal information into questions. We keep your questions for 90 days to apply the daily limit and to improve the answers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The app itself is served by GitHub Pages. Market data and news come from Alpaca; your personal data is not sent to them.

Your choices

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time by contacting the support e-mail shown on the sign-in screen; we delete it within 30 days. Signing out removes the session from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
